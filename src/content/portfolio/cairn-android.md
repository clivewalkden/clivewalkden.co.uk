Cairn is a body-measurement tracker for Android and Wear OS, built around Android's Health Connect
platform rather than an account or a cloud service. Weigh-ins, body composition and heart rate are
logged straight into Health Connect from the phone or the watch, and the dashboard reads back
whatever the watch has already recorded for steps, calories, exercise and sleep — so everything
lives in one place without Cairn ever needing to collect it itself.

## What it does

**Dashboard at a glance** — weight, BMI, body fat %, body water %, current heart rate and last
night's sleep, all on one screen, backed by Health Connect rather than a private database.

**Wear OS quick-log** — add a weigh-in from the watch app without reaching for a phone.

**Continuous heart rate, on your terms** — optional watch-driven monitoring with today's min,
average and max plus a full readings graph, switched on and off from the watch itself.

**History that's actually readable** — every past measurement and heart-rate session in one list.

## Privacy by design

Cairn has no account, no analytics and makes no network calls of any kind — there is no server for
your data to reach in the first place. See the [Cairn app page](/apps/cairn/) for screenshots and
the [full privacy policy](/apps/cairn/privacy/) for a record-by-record breakdown of what it reads
and writes.
